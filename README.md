### Juan del Mazo Vázquez

**Engineer by training, analyst by trade.** I connect data, business and software: I can build the tool, analyse the data and explain the result to the people who decide.

- Junior Business Analyst at **Bankinter** (Madrid), in the pre-approved consumer loans analysis team.
- Previously a software developer at **Idaero Solutions**, building software for the aerospace industry: I led the visualisation interface of a digital twin for composite manufacturing.
- MSc in Big Data Analytics, Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (first-class honours). BSc in Computer Engineering, Universidad Politécnica de Madrid, with an Erasmus year at the University of Bristol.

**Selected work**

| Project | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Lead-Lag Trading Strategy](https://github.com/JuandelMazoVazquez/Lead-Lag-Trading-Strategy) | MSc thesis. Which U.S. stocks move first? DTW and spectral clustering on 500 stocks (2000–2019); best model Sharpe 0.596 vs 0.442 for the cross-correlation baseline. |
| [Shuffl](https://github.com/JuandelMazoVazquez/new-spoti) | A truly uniform shuffle for Spotify playlists. Static PWA, no server, unbiased Fisher–Yates, unit and end-to-end tests in CI. |
| [Optimisation under uncertainty](https://github.com/JuandelMazoVazquez/Advanced-Optimization-Nonlinear-Uncertainty) | Nonlinear solvers benchmarked on regularised regression and a two-stage stochastic model in Pyomo (EVPI, VSS). |
| [Sales forecasting](https://github.com/JuandelMazoVazquez/Sales-Prediction-Advanced-Regression) | Weekly sales from marketing spend in R, with time-aware cross-validation and a weighted ensemble. |
| [Employee attrition](https://github.com/JuandelMazoVazquez/Employee-Attrition-Prediction-ML) | Imbalanced classification with SMOTE, Optuna and SHAP; 0.92 balanced accuracy on test. |
